Officers photograph a pack, the app verifies every mandatory declaration against the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules on-device — then files the report here, in the shared register.

Front panel, MRP close-up, dates, care block. OCR and rule-checks run on the phone — no signal needed.

Each finished report syncs in the background: photos, verdict, score and per-rule evidence.

Supervisors search files, study violations, download archival PDFs and the Excel sheet.

Checked against the 2011 rules

LM-R6-NAME · LM-R6-COMMON · LM-R6-NETQ · LM-R6-DATE-MFG · LM-R6-DATE-EXP · LM-R6-MRP · LM-R6-MRP-TAX · LM-R9-SINGLE-MRP · LM-R6-CARE · LM-R6-ORIGIN · LM-R6-BATCH · LM-FOOD-FSSAI · LM-READ-QUALITY · LM-R7-FONT

Fail means a proven violation on a readable pack. Unverified means retake the photo — never a penalty for a weak capture.
